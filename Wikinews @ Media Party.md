A more universal, data-driven version of https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Current\_events . The portal will have community-edited, generated headlines displaying a variety of daily events from the rich data on Wikipedia and Wikidata.

Will link out to relevant updated Wikipedia articles and the discussions around them, a strong emphasis on journalistic outlets being cited by Wikipedia, as well as original reporting from volunteer communities.

The previous wikinews.org project is being closed down by the Wikimedia Foundation, and this is a reconceptualization being stewarded by Wikimedia NYC.
